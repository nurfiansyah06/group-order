# Table tab

User direction: a playful paper receipt, for friends splitting a meal.

Dial: ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 1.

- Cream paper and dark brown ink recall a diner receipt; tomato red highlights the main action.
- Georgia headings give the table a friendly menu voice; Trebuchet keeps form fields readable; small Courier totals align with printed receipts.
- A working editor beside a receipt keeps inputs and consequences together; narrow screens place the receipt after the editor.
- Dashed separators and a zigzag paper edge repeat the receipt identity, not an unrelated background pattern.
- The receipt alone has a shadow to distinguish the paper from the tabletop.
- Section spacing groups the meal setup, item entry, and extras; no motion beyond interaction feedback.
- Fixed light theme preserves the paper-receipt direction.
- Text-only product name; no generated visual assets or invented example data.
